## The two reviews

Each review is performed by an agent that did not write the documents, with a
brief that asks it to **refute**, not to confirm. They work from different
perspectives:

* **Review A -- truth against evidence.** For every figure and every
  empirical sentence: does the named source say exactly this, at the commit
  being published? Are dated historical figures still presented as dated? Is
  any development state worded as a release claim? Is anything marked pending
  presented as proven? Is the integrity gate's scope stated no wider than the
  reviewers' own?
* **Review B -- what a reader is told, and what is left out.** Is any current
  limitation hidden or softened -- in particular the quota misclassification,
  the first-prompt race, the role that can be re-woken, and the red board? Do
  the new sections contradict the unchanged `v0.1.0` text without saying so?
  Are the five steps -- candidate creation, product acceptance, integration
  acceptance, merge authority, phase closure -- kept apart everywhere? Would a
  reader of the export be sent to a path the export does not carry?
